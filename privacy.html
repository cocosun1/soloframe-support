中文

视频在设备上处理

SoloFrame 在你的设备上分析所选视频并生成直拍。正常制作过程不会将视频、画面或人物特征上传给开发者。App 不提供账号登录，也没有广告或第三方行为分析服务。

你选择提供的视频

你通过系统照片或文件选择器选择视频。App 在自己的空间内处理副本，不修改原视频。保存到照片需要添加权限；也可以保存到文件。系统照片或文件服务可能需要下载云端素材，这不等于 SoloFrame 将素材上传给开发者。

当前任务与本机记录

App 保存当前任务和少量本机处理记录，用于恢复任务和排查故障。结束任务或成功开始新任务后，会清理 App 内不再使用的任务文件；你已保存到照片或文件的副本不受影响。工作缓存可能被系统清理，请主动保存想保留的成片。

TestFlight 与主动反馈

首轮测试使用 TestFlight 自带反馈。Apple 按其 TestFlight 隐私说明处理测试、崩溃和主动提交的反馈信息。提交的反馈会提供给 Apple 和开发者；本机处理不代表 Apple 的测试服务不处理数据。

反馈可以只写文字，截图可选，无需提供原视频。请发送前检查截图中他人的信息。通过邮件联系时，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址、正文及你主动附上的附件。反馈只用于排查问题和改进产品，不用于广告、模型训练或公开传播。若排查问题确需视频，会另行说明用途、接收方、留存及删除方式，并征求同意。

反馈附件副本的删除

开发者另行下载或保存用于排查问题的反馈附件副本（例如截图，以及经你另行同意提供的原视频或成片），会在对应问题关闭后 30 天内删除。删除由开发者负责，你无需主动申请；也可通过下方邮箱要求提前删除。可以保留不含个人素材及可识别个人信息的问题摘要。这项规则不删除你手机里的原片或已保存的作品，也不代表开发者能够控制 Apple 独立保存的信息。

网站访问

本说明网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。我们未加入网站统计、广告或上传表单。访问网页时，托管服务会接收连接所需的技术信息；其处理方式请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。这不向网站提供你在 App 中选择的视频。

English

Video processing on your device

SoloFrame analyzes your selected video and creates the solo on your device. Normal creation does not upload videos, frames, or appearance features to the developer. The app has no account sign-in, advertising, or third-party behavioral analytics service.

Videos you choose to provide

You choose a video through the system Photos or Files picker. The app processes a local copy without modifying your original. Saving to Photos requires permission to add videos; saving to Files is also available. A system photo or file provider may download cloud-hosted media. This is separate from SoloFrame sending media to the developer.

Current task and local records

The app keeps the current task and limited local processing records for recovery and troubleshooting. Ending a task or successfully starting a new one removes inactive app-owned task files. Copies you saved to Photos or Files are unaffected. The system may remove working caches, so save results you want to keep.

TestFlight and voluntary feedback

The first beta uses TestFlight’s built-in feedback. Apple processes testing, crash, and voluntarily submitted feedback information under its TestFlight privacy notice. Submitted feedback is provided to Apple and the developer. On-device processing does not mean Apple’s testing service processes no data.

Text-only feedback is welcome; screenshots are optional and original footage is not required. Check screenshots for other people’s information before sending. If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Feedback is used only for troubleshooting and product improvement, not advertising, model training, or public sharing. Any separate request for video will explain its purpose, recipient, retention, and deletion arrangements and ask for your consent.

Deleting copies of feedback attachments

Copies of feedback attachments separately downloaded or saved by the developer for troubleshooting (such as screenshots, or original or exported videos provided with your separate consent) will be deleted within 30 days after the corresponding issue is closed. The developer is responsible for deletion; you do not need to request it, but may request earlier deletion using the email below. Issue summaries without personal media or identifying information may be retained. This rule does not delete originals or saved results on your device and does not imply control over information Apple retains independently.

Individual publisher: 孙雨彤. For support, privacy questions, or requests about deleting submitted feedback, contact cocosun268@gmail.com.

Website visits

Visiting does not provide this website with videos you select in the app.
