如何反馈

  1. 打开 TestFlight，进入 SoloFrame 页面，选择“Send Beta Feedback”。
  2. 描述发生了什么、在哪一步，以及视频中的大约秒数。可不附截图；如附截图，请先检查其中的个人信息。
  3. 补充构建号、iPhone机型、iOS版本、素材时长，以及是否切到后台。无需原视频、文件名或完整诊断日志。

反馈由 Apple 和开发者接收。操作详情见 Apple TestFlight 指南；支持或隐私问题也可直接发送邮件。App 内没有单独的反馈上传入口。

成功也欢迎反馈：请记录总尝试数、已生成且保存的次数，以及愿意保留的成片数。重试另算一次；失败、格式拒绝与主动取消分开记录。

可选识歌

识歌默认关闭，可在 App 的“隐私与支持”了解数据处理并主动开启。开启后，仅在之后主动开始生成、且 App 在前台时尝试联网识别。原视频和原始音频不发送给识歌服务。歌曲可能识别不到或识别有误；没有歌曲卡片不等于视频制作失败。同一任务和范围不会因重开页面、恢复网络或重试生成而自动重复识歌。你仍可正常保存视频；识歌不授予音乐使用权。

关闭会阻止后续请求，已发送请求无法撤回。详情见 隐私说明。如反馈识歌问题，请提供是否已开启、网络状态、构建号及现象，无需原片或音频。

先用常见短片试试

首轮建议使用一分钟以内、自己有权使用的多人课堂视频。SoloFrame 当前要求 iPhone、iOS 18或更新版本；不代表所有机型和系统均已验证。

  • 原视频大于0秒且不超过600秒，显示宽高比必须大于9:16。9:16或更窄会被拒绝；横屏、方形、4:5仍须通过其他格式检查。
  • 优先使用普通SDR素材。受支持HDR需要确认转为SDR，成片不保留HDR效果，原片不变；其他格式可能被拒绝。
  • 后台分析可能很慢。HDR转换和最后生成需要留在前台，切后台可能中止并需重试。
  • 保存到照片需要添加权限，也可以保存到文件。请及时保存想保留的成片，App 预览不是永久作品库。

若原片或已保存成片受损、出现意外传输，或App无法恢复操作，请停止使用该构建并通过上述邮箱联系；不要首先卸载。

English

Send feedback

  1. Open TestFlight, select SoloFrame, and choose “Send Beta Feedback.”
  2. Describe the symptom, step, and approximate video timestamp. A screenshot is optional; check it for personal information before sending.
  3. Include the build, iPhone model, iOS version, video duration, and whether the app was backgrounded. Original footage, filenames, and raw diagnostic logs are not required.

Apple and the developer receive the feedback. See Apple’s TestFlight instructions. You can also email support or privacy questions. There is no separate feedback upload feature inside SoloFrame.

Successful attempts are helpful too: record total attempts, how many generated and saved successfully, and how many results you would keep. Count a retry separately, and distinguish failures, format rejections, and voluntary cancellations.

Optional song identification

Identification is off by default. Read the disclosure in Privacy & Support in the app and choose whether to enable it. Once enabled, it attempts online identification only when you later start generation with the app in the foreground. Original video and raw audio are not sent to the recognition service. A song may be unidentified or incorrect; a missing song card does not mean video creation failed. Reopening a screen, restoring connectivity, or retrying generation does not automatically identify the same task and range again. You can still save the video. Recognition does not grant music usage rights.

Turning it off blocks subsequent requests but cannot recall requests already sent. See the privacy notice. To report an identification issue, describe whether it was enabled, connectivity, build, and symptom; original footage or audio is not required.

Start with a familiar short video

For the first round, use group classroom footage you have permission to use, preferably under one minute. SoloFrame currently requires an iPhone running iOS 18 or later. This does not mean every model or OS version has been validated.

  • The original must be longer than zero and no more than 600 seconds, with a displayed width-to-height ratio greater than 9:16. Videos at 9:16 or narrower are rejected. Landscape, square, and 4:5 still need to pass other format checks.
  • Start with ordinary SDR footage. Supported HDR requires consent to convert to SDR; the result loses HDR effects and the original is unchanged. Other formats may be rejected.
  • Background analysis can be very slow. HDR conversion and final generation require the foreground; leaving the app may interrupt them and require a retry.
  • Saving to Photos requires add permission; saving to Files is also available. Save results you want to keep, as in-app previews are not a permanent library.

If an original or saved result is damaged, an unexpected transfer occurs, or the app becomes unrecoverably stuck, stop using the build and contact the email above. Avoid uninstalling as your first response.
